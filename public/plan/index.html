The Strategic Frame Why this launch matters

Not a new product.
A new identity.

A second product changes what Lumen5 is. One product is a tool. Two products is a platform. That shift is the most valuable thing about this launch — and the messaging should lean into it deliberately.

Today
"AI video tool"
A crowded category. Dozens of competitors on price, speed, and novelty. Hard to defend, hard to charge enterprise money for, easy to be compared against a $29/month alternative.
Commodity Single use Price-pressured
→
After launch
"Video platform"
A suite serious content teams build on. Stickier, more enterprise-credible, harder to displace. The two-product moment is the proof point — Lumen5 isn't one trick, it's where B2B video lives.
Enterprise-credible Sticky Defensible
Every asset in this campaign carries that frame quietly underneath. The launch isn't "we made an explainer tool." It's "we became a platform — and here's the first proof." Website and GA messaging should make the platform move on purpose.

The Hero Video, Wireframed Storyboard

On camera.
Then product.
Cut. Repeat.

The format you flagged: a real person on camera, lower-third name and title, intercut with product and animation. Talking head sets up the why, the cutaway shows the how, cut back to land it. Here's the reference, then the shot-by-shot board.

Reference · format we want
What to steal from it

The "expert explains, product demonstrates" format.

  • Lower third: name and role in a clean serif, bottom-left. Signals a real builder, not a spokesperson.
  • Cut rhythm: talking head for the why, animation for the how, back to land the point. Energy stays up.
  • Environment: real office, soft depth of field. Warm and cinematic, not a sterile studio.
  • Restraint: no hype, no music swells. The person and the product carry it.

Rollout Strategy Phased launch plan

Soft now.
Full ham
in September.

August is a graveyard for enterprise attention. We use it to build the runway — existing customers, proof assets, campaign infrastructure. September is when we open the throttle.

The logic "August is dead" is a real phenomenon in B2B. Decision-makers are on holiday. Budgets aren't moving. Any hard launch in August gets half the eyeballs and costs the same. So we soft launch to the existing customer base (the easiest conversion), capture the proof we need, and go full push in September when enterprise buyers are back at their desks with Q4 budget conversations starting.
